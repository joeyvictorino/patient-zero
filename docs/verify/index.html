      What a good result means. The files are the ones that were signed, and nothing was added, removed or edited afterward.

      What it does not mean. It does not mean the vendor is HIPAA compliant, and it is not a certification; none exists. It does not show that the claims in the files are true, only that they have not changed. Read the numbers above: "confirmed by a person" is the vendor's own word unless someone independent co-signed, and "tested clean only" means software found nothing, which is weak evidence.

      This page is part of patient-zero, an open source project. It works offline: you can save it and open it from your own computer.
