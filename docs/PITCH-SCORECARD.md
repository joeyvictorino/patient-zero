# Scorecard for a health AI pitch

Generated by `pz brief judge`. Do not edit by hand.

For judges, mentors and buyers hearing a pitch from a product that will touch patient information. Score each line 0, 1 or 2. It measures whether the team knows what it has and has not done, which predicts more than polish does. A team that says "we have not done that yet, here is the plan" earns a 1. A team that says "we're HIPAA compliant" and cannot say who checked earns a 0.

## 1. Where does patient data go?

| Score | What earns it |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Names every company that receives it, including the AI model provider, without being asked twice. |
| 1 | Not done yet, with a specific plan and date. |
| 0 | Does not know, or claims it without being able to say who checked. |

Safeguards behind this question: TRUST-1.

Sixteen is the top score. A low score early is normal and fixable. The point of the card is that the same eight questions are asked of every team, and that nobody leaves believing a badge on a slide is evidence.
