# The care-claims scan is a phrase list

`pz scan --care-claims` matches wording that presents an AI as a therapist or as mental health care. It is easy to evade, it misjudges some sentences, and it reads only the text it is given, not what the product does. Nevada AB 406 also bars systems programmed to provide such care, which no text scan can determine.

What would close this: a reviewed corpus of real marketing copy with known outcomes, and guidance from Nevada counsel on borderline wording.
