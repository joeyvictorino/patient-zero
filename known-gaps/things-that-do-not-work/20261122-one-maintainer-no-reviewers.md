# The governance is written down but there is nobody in it yet

`GOVERNANCE.md` describes reviewers per family and decisions by a majority of maintainers. Today there is one maintainer and every reviewer seat in `MAINTAINERS.md` is open. Changes to the standard are therefore reviewed by the person who wrote them. The name is not a registered trademark and the project has no neutral home.

What would close this: a second maintainer from another organization, a filled reviewer seat for each family (the CONSENT and Nevada seats first), and a decision on registration and on a home for the standard.
